# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel

Customers cancel from Account Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Support agents can also
cancel on a customer's behalf after verifying identity.

## When cancellation takes effect

Cancellation always takes effect at the end of the current billing period, never immediately. The
customer retains full access to paid features until that date.

## Refunds on cancellation

Cancelling does not, by itself, trigger a refund. The customer already paid for access through
the end of the current billing period and that access is not reduced. See `refund_policy.md` for
the separate rules on renewal-charge refunds.

## Reactivating

A cancelled account can be reactivated any time before the end of the current billing period at no
extra charge. After that date, reactivating requires a new subscription purchase.

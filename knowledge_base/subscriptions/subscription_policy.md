# Subscription Policy

## Plan tiers

- **Free**: core features, single user, community support only.
- **Pro**: full features, single user, email support, billed monthly or annually.
- **Enterprise**: full features, multiple users, dedicated support, billed annually only.

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount for the remainder of
the current billing cycle at the new plan's rate.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, not immediately. The customer
keeps their current plan's features until then. Downgrades issue a partial refund only if the
downgrade is from Enterprise (annual billing) — see `refund_process.md`.

## Annual vs monthly billing

Annual billing is discounted 20% compared to monthly billing for the same plan. Switching from
monthly to annual takes effect at the next renewal date, not immediately.

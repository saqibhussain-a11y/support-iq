# Account Security

## Two-factor authentication

2FA is optional but strongly recommended. It can be enabled under Account Settings > Security. If
a customer is locked out after enabling 2FA and has lost their device, this must be escalated to
account security for identity verification — support agents cannot disable 2FA directly.

## Suspicious activity or suspected fraud

Any report of unauthorized account access, a stolen or compromised card, or unrecognized charges
combined with account changes the customer did not make must be escalated immediately to the
security team, regardless of the time of day. This is the only category of issue that bypasses
normal support queue prioritization entirely.

## Changing the email on file

Requires the customer to confirm their current password and click a confirmation link sent to the
new email address. Support agents cannot change the email on file on a customer's behalf.

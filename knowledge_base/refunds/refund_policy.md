# Refund Policy

## Eligibility window

Refunds may be requested within 30 days of the original charge date. Requests made after 30 days
are not eligible for a refund under any circumstances, including billing disputes.

## What is refundable

- Confirmed duplicate or erroneous charges: always refundable in full, regardless of usage.
- First-time subscription purchases: refundable in full if requested within the 30-day window,
  regardless of how much the product was used.
- Renewal charges (i.e., not your first payment): refundable only if the account had less than 10
  minutes of usage after the renewal date.

## What is not refundable

- Charges older than 30 days.
- Renewal charges on accounts with more than 10 minutes of post-renewal usage.
- Any charge where the customer has already received a refund for the same billing period.

## Who approves refunds

Refunds for confirmed duplicate charges and first-time purchases within the window can be approved
directly by a support agent. Any other refund request must be escalated for manual review — see
`escalation_policy.md`.

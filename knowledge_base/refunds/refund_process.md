# Refund Process

## How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are always returned to the original payment method used for the charge. We
cannot issue refunds as account credit, gift card, or to a different card.

## How long it takes

Once approved, a refund is submitted to the payment processor within 1 business day. From there,
it typically takes 5-7 business days to appear on the customer's statement, depending on their
bank. We cannot expedite this — it is controlled by the card issuer, not by us.

## What the customer sees

The customer receives an email confirmation the moment the refund is approved, and a second email
once the processor confirms the funds were released. The refunded amount will show as a separate
line item, not a reversal of the original charge.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds are only issued for prorated mid-cycle plan downgrades. All other approved refunds
are for the full charge amount.

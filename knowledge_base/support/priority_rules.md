# Priority Rules

## High priority

Any issue involving money already charged (billing disputes, refund requests, duplicate charges)
or any security escalation. High-priority tickets are reviewed within 1 business day.

## Medium priority

Account access issues that do not involve suspected fraud (password resets that need escalation,
2FA lockouts) and subscription changes with unclear customer intent.

## Low priority

General how-to questions, plan tier explanations, and any request fully answered by resolving
automatically from documentation.

## Sentiment does not change priority by itself

A frustrated or angry tone does not, on its own, raise priority — the underlying issue category
does. A calmly worded billing dispute is still high priority; an angrily worded how-to question is
still low priority. Sentiment is tracked separately so agents know how to phrase their response,
not to determine queue order.

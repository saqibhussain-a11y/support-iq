# Escalation Policy

## Resolve automatically

Issues that match a documented, unambiguous policy answer with no account-specific judgment call
required: password reset instructions, explaining a charge, explaining plan tiers, general
how-to questions. These can be answered directly from policy documentation without human review.

## Escalate for human review

Billing disputes that require judgment (e.g., a renewal refund request outside the automatic
duplicate-charge case), refund requests that fall outside the documented eligibility window edge
cases, and any request where the customer explicitly disputes a documented policy. These are
routed to the billing team, who reviews within 1 business day.

## Escalate immediately

Any suspected fraud, unauthorized account access, or a customer reporting a stolen payment method
must be escalated immediately to the security team, bypassing the normal queue. Do not attempt to
resolve these from policy documentation alone, and do not wait for the standard 1 business day
review window.

## Examples

- "How do I reset my password?" → Resolve automatically.
- "I was charged twice." → Resolve automatically if it matches the documented duplicate-charge
  process; escalate for human review only if the charges don't match that pattern.
- "I think someone stole my card." → Escalate immediately.

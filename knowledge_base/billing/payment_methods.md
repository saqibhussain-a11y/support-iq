# Payment Methods

## Updating your card

Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Method to add or replace a card. The new card is used
starting with your next billing cycle; it does not retroactively charge for the current cycle.

## What happens when a payment fails?

If a scheduled charge fails, we retry automatically after 24 hours, then again after 72 hours. If
all three attempts fail, the account is moved to "Past Due" status and access to paid features is
suspended until a valid payment method is added. Past Due accounts are not cancelled automatically
until 14 days of nonpayment have passed.

## Can I have more than one payment method on file?

No. Each account has exactly one active payment method at a time. Adding a new card replaces the
previous one.

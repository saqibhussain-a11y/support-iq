# Billing FAQ

## When am I charged?

Subscriptions are billed in advance on the same day of the month you first subscribed. If you
subscribed on the 31st and a month has fewer days, you are billed on the last day of that month.

## What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit and debit cards. We do not accept PayPal,
wire transfers, or cryptocurrency.

## Why does my invoice show a different amount than expected?

Invoices can differ from the base plan price for three reasons: a mid-cycle plan upgrade
(prorated), applicable sales tax based on your billing address, or a previously applied coupon
that has expired.

## How do I get a copy of my invoice?

Invoices are emailed automatically after every successful charge. Past invoices are also available
under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History.
